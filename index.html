About

I build systems that span the stack — from PHP/Laravel web applications down to ESP8266 sensor hardware. I'm a 4th-year BSIT student at Iloilo State University of Fisheries Science and Technology (ISUFST), Dingle Campus.

Most of my project work sits where ISUFST's fisheries focus meets software engineering: an IoT water quality monitor for fishponds, a livestock management platform for the local gamefowl industry, and a set of Laravel systems built for coursework and department use.

Outside coursework, I build gaming PCs and have hands-on experience assembling e-bikes, including Surron, Ride Pro, and Ultra Bee models.
